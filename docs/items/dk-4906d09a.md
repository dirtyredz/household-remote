# Separate TV message log, redaction and IME matching from RemoteSession


Source: v0.2.6 push review (componentization and abstraction lenses, 2026-10-30), P2.

- RemoteSession owns the TvMessageLog instance and the IME start/end matching with its deferred-start flag, on top of recovery. Direction: inject the log or an observer from the composition root, and extract a small ImeEvents helper that received() delegates to.
- TvMessageLog mixes bounded history, the redaction policy (sanitize, scrub, regexes) and Samsung keyboard-frame knowledge (TypeOfRemote filtering, the Cmd/base64 special case). Its public contract is preformatted strings. Direction: extract a MessageRedactor; move keyboard-frame recognition into SamsungProtocol; store a LoggedMessage(time, direction, json, decodedText) and render it in the UI.
- SettingsDialog holds its own sub-navigation (showMessages) next to RemoteApp Overlay. Direction: when a third settings page appears, add Overlay.TvMessages or a SettingsRoute.

Acceptance: no behaviour change; the fast gate passes.
